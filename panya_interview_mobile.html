Interview Playbook

AI is the easy part. Knowing what the client needs — that's the job.

00 · 先看这里

这不是知识小抄,是叙事脚本。

面试官考的是分析拆解和讲解能力,英文顺带验。重点不是懂多少 AI 名词,而是能不能把方案讲清楚、说服客户。

压住一个认知: 这岗是「AI 应用售前顾问」,JD 白纸黑字写 "无需底层算法、模型研发"。它本来就不要你做模型工程——要你听懂业务、设计方案、算效益、讲清楚。每一项都踩在你强项上。你不是扬短,是扬长。
你的三张牌:① 懂客户真业务(尤其 BFSI);② 已动手做过 SME Loan 风控 AI 探索(非零经验);③ 有把项目拆开讲明白的框架。
01 · 人设地基

你站台上,到底是谁?

你是那个能把 AI 翻译成客户买得起、落得了地的方案的人。

01 — I speak the client's language

从业务出发,不从名词出发

多年 BFSI 甲方。我坐过客户的位子,懂他们的痛点、KPI、顾虑。售前最大的失败是"方案很炫客户用不上"——我天然从业务反推技术。

02 — Already on the road

我已经动手用过 AI

做过 Global Bank SME Loan 风控 workflow 探索。不是"听说过",是"已经把 AI 用在真实业务里"——能在应用层快速上手。

03 — Break it down & sell it

把复杂讲简单

给我一个机会,我能拆成清晰落地路径:场景→方案→架构→效益→风险。技术认可、高管点头——这就是售前核心。

Beat 1 · Open~50s

Thank you. I want to start with a confession, and then a claim.

The confession: I'm not the person here who has built the most AI models. The claim: I might be the one who best understands why most AI projects fail to land — and how to make sure ours don't.

They rarely fail on the technology. They fail when the solution doesn't match what the client actually needs. And matching technology to real business need — that's exactly what I've done for years on the BFSI side.

▷ 怎么讲›

别道歉式心虚。"confession + claim" 是钩子——坦诚弱点立刻翻盘。说 "why most AI projects fail to land" 时放慢、加重,看着负责人眼睛。这句直接点出售前的核心价值,定全场基调。

Beat 2 · How I see AI~110s

How do I understand AI? My one-line version:

An LLM is a brilliant, fast, confident intern who has read everything and remembers nothing about the client.

Brilliant — it reasons over language like nothing before. Fast — seconds. Confident — and that's the danger: it gives a wrong answer in the same tone as a right one. And it remembers nothing about the client.

So the model is never the hard part. It's the intern. The real work is around it: the right knowledge — RAG; the right instructions — prompt engineering; the right tools — integration; the right guardrails — risk.

That's the game in pre-sales: not "look what AI can do," but "here's how it solves your problem, safely, and the value it returns."

▷ 怎么讲›

全场最重要的 90 秒。"实习生"比喻是讲解能力的硬证据。说 RAG / 提示词 / 集成 / 风险时用手在空中点四下,制造"我有结构"。最后一句是售前灵魂——重重落下。这四个词正好对应 JD 任职要求里的"应用层技术"。

Beat 3 · The pain I know~90s

Let me make this concrete with something I've actually worked on.

I explored an AI-assisted workflow for SME loan risk control at a global bank. Not production — an exploration. But it taught me what matters most:

The hard part was never making the model sound smart. It was the scoping — where AI genuinely added value, where it didn't, and where it created more risk than it removed. Knowing what not to automate is half the job.

That's the pre-sales instinct. My value isn't the flashiest demo. It's saying: "For this, AI gives clear return. For that, honestly, not yet — let's not waste your budget." That honesty earns trust. I've been on their side of the table.

▷ 怎么讲›

"软化零经验"主攻段。大方承认 "an exploration, not production" 反而可信。杀招:把风控里学到的"判断哪里该用 AI"迁移成售前能力,正中 JD"可行性评估、把控风险"。说 "let's not waste your budget" 时带笃定微笑——甲方视角才说得出。

Beat 4 · My breakdown~120s

Now the part you asked me to show — how I break a problem down. Any AI opportunity, I run through five steps:

One — understand the business: the real pain, the KPI behind it. Two — design the solution: which capability fits, how it plugs in. Three — map the landing path: phased, pilot to production. Four — prove the value: cost-benefit in their numbers. Five — manage the risk: where it goes wrong, answered before they ask.

I'm strongest at one, four, and five. Steps two and three lean technical — that's where I work shoulder-to-shoulder with the delivery and tech team. I bring the "what the client needs," they bring the "how we build it."

▷ 怎么讲›

五步直接呼应 JD 职责 1-3——"我研究过这岗"的硬证据。报五条要有节奏,像数拍子。最后一句关键:坦白把偏技术的 2/3 交给技术团队,正中负责人"你 focus 面客、新同学负责技术"的分工设想。

Beat 5 · Plan + Close~100s

How do I get productive fast? Three phases.

First 30 days — learn our portfolio and reference cases, shadow real client conversations. My BFSI background means I'm not learning the client side from scratch.

Days 30–60 — learn AI-native, contribute AI-native. I use these tools daily to draft docs, build value models, pressure-test proposals. I co-own a real opportunity and start carrying the client-facing explanation.

Days 60–90 — lead from the front. Take point on an engagement end to end, measured by a proposal that moves forward.

To close: you don't need me to be the deepest AI engineer — this role doesn't either. You need someone who understands the client, shapes AI into something they'll buy, and learns at the speed this field moves. That's the person I am — and the person I'm becoming faster every week. Thank you.

▷ 怎么讲›

计划要"可执行"不空谈。"learn AI-native, contribute AI-native" 是 AI-Native 人设点睛句,重读。"this role doesn't either" 巧妙呼应 JD"无需底层算法",暗示你读懂岗位本质。首尾呼应 confession/claim。说完——停,微笑,不补话。

03 · 五步框架速查

逐条对上 JD。

追问"具体怎么做方案"时调出来。★ 是你的主场。

1

Understand the Business ★ 主场

JD #2 · 梳理痛点与数字化需求

搞清真痛点、背后 KPI、客户眼中的"成功"。甲方视角值钱。

2

Design the Solution

JD #1 · 设计可落地应用方案

选对能力(文本/语音/视觉/多模态),想清集成。联动技术团队。

3

Map the Landing Path

JD #2+#4 · 落地路径 · 跟进实施

试点到生产的分阶段路线,让客户不必赌一把。你定节奏和验收。

4

Prove the Value ★ 主场

JD #2 · 效益分析

用客户的数字算成本-收益。说服高管买单的关键,你懂商业语言。

5

Manage the Risk ★ 主场

JD #3 · 可行性 · 把控风险

哪里会出问题、客户问之前备好答案。你做过风控,天然直觉。

04 · 追问应答

他大概会问这些。

点开看思路。原则:不装懂、往你主场引、用比喻兜底。

"你缺 AI 经验,怎么信你跟得上?"+
不慌、不过度承诺。 "Two reasons. I've already started — the SME loan exploration wasn't theory, I built it. And the expensive gap here isn't AI engineering, which the job doesn't require — it's understanding the client's business, which I already have. I'd rather be the consultant who knows the client and picks up the tooling, than the reverse."
"客户问:为什么用 RAG 不直接微调?"+
考你能否讲给客户听,用实习生比喻。 "Fine-tuning changes how the intern thinks — expensive, slow, risky when policies change. RAG changes what it can look up — we feed it your current docs at question time. For most enterprise cases RAG lands faster, costs less, stays auditable — you trace exactly which document an answer came from." 讲清比讲全重要。
"给新客户做方案,你怎么 scope?"+
送分题——用五步框架。 "First, dig into their real pain and KPI — not the AI they think they want. Second, match capability and integration. Third, a phased path. Fourth, the value case in their numbers. And throughout, honest about risk — including where AI isn't the answer yet. That last part earns trust and closes deals." 报五步要有节奏。
"另一新同学做技术,你俩怎么分工?"+
负责人的真实关切。 "Cleanly. I own the client-facing layer — business, value case, explaining the solution, stakeholder alignment. They own the technical depth — architecture, integration, build. The overlap is the fun part: I bring 'what the client needs and why', they bring 'how it works', I translate it into something they buy." 答好=告诉他你懂团队结构。
"你实际用过哪些 AI 工具?"+
诚实 + AI-Native。 只说真用过的(Claude/ChatGPT),别编没碰过的工具。"I use LLM tools daily — drafting, structuring proposals, learning faster. I understand the application-layer pieces — prompt engineering, RAG, vector databases — and I'm actively going deeper. I'd rather tell you honestly where I am than oversell it." 诚实本身就是顾问看重的品质。
"卖 AI 方案最难的是什么?"+
展示你懂售前本质。 "Managing expectations. Clients expect magic or trust nothing. My job is the realistic middle — show what AI genuinely does for their case, be upfront about limits, back it with a value case they believe. Deals that stick aren't won on the best demo — on the most honest, well-scoped plan." 答好=天生做售前的料。

明天之前,做这几件。
